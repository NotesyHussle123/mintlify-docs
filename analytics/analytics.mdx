**Analytics** shows what is sold through chat, how busy your inbox is, and how quickly your team replies.

<Note>
Managers, admins and owners only. Analytics is included in the Growth and Business plans, and in the free trial. On Starter the page shows what you would get by moving up; your conversations are counted in the meantime.
</Note>

## Choose the period and filters

The bar at the top of the page sets what every figure below it covers.

- **Date range**: **Last 7 days** (the default), **Last 30 days**, **Last 90 days**, **This month**, **Last month** or **Custom range**. A custom range can cover up to 366 days and cannot end after today.
- **Team member**: one person's work. Shown when there is more than one person.
- **Channel**: WhatsApp or website chat. Shown when you have both.
- **WhatsApp number**: shown when you have more than one number.

Days are counted in your workspace's time zone, and today is included. The filters are kept in the page's address, so you can share a filtered view as a link.

## How things are counted

- A conversation somebody **deleted** is left out of every figure. **Archived** conversations still count.
- A broadcast does not create a new conversation for each recipient. It counts once the customer answers.
- A message belongs to the person who **sent** it, not to whoever the chat is assigned to today. Reassigning a chat does not move its history.
- An automatic message, such as one from a workflow, never counts as a reply from a person.

## The figures

Each tile has a line beneath it saying what it counts. Where there is an earlier period of the same length to compare with, the tile shows the change.

| Figure | What it counts |
| --- | --- |
| **Sales through chat** | Payment links paid in the period, in your workspace's currency. Payments in another currency are listed beneath and left out of the total. |
| **New conversations** | Chats that began in the period. |
| **Median first response** | The time from a customer's first message to the first reply a person sent. |
| **Active reps** | Team members who sent at least one message in the period, out of everyone on the team. |
| **Sent by your team** | Messages a person sent, from Companion or from their phone. |
| **Sent automatically** | Messages from workflows, website chat greetings and broadcasts. |
| **Messages received** | Messages customers sent you. |
| **Conversations answered** | Of the chats where a customer first wrote in the period, the share a person has replied to. |

## The charts

- **Sales through chat** and **Messages sent by your team** show one bar for each day. Longer periods are grouped into weeks or months. The bar that includes today is shown paler, because it is still filling up.
- **Busiest hours** and **Busiest days** show when customers write to you, by hour of the day and by day of the week.

## Team leaderboard

One row for each current team member, ranked by sales.

| Column | What it counts |
| --- | --- |
| **Conversations replied in** | Different conversations they sent a message in. |
| **Messages sent** | Messages they sent. |
| **Sales** | Paid payment links that they created. |
| **Median first response** | For the first replies that were theirs. |

Work by people who have since left is added together in one row, **Others, no longer on the team**. The **Total** row counts each conversation once, so it can be less than the column above it when two people replied in the same chat.

## Response times

Three meters under the leaderboard show the share of conversations answered, and the share that got a first reply within 5 minutes and within an hour. All three are counted from each customer's first message.

## Payment links and broadcasts

- **Payment links**: links sent in the period, how many were opened and paid, the conversion rate, and the average order value.
- **Broadcasts**: broadcasts that began sending in the period, with recipients, delivered, read and replied. This card is hidden when you filter by website chat.

## Channels and tags

- **Channels** shows where new conversations began, WhatsApp or website chat, with messages sent and received on each. It appears when you have both and have not filtered by one.
- **Top tags** shows the tags on conversations that began in the period.

## Download the figures

**Download CSV** at the top of the page gives you the daily figures. **Download CSV** on the leaderboard gives you the figures for each person. Both use the period and filters you have set.
