## Replying and templates

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I cannot type a reply to this customer">
    The box where you would type says which of these it is.

    - **More than 24 hours have passed since the customer last wrote.** WhatsApp only carries an approved template until they write again. Choose **Send a template**.
    - **Typed replies are switched off for your workspace.** Reply from the WhatsApp Business app on your phone, or send a template or another action. An owner or admin can switch typed replies back on under **Settings**, **General**.
    - **Nothing can be sent at all.** The number is paused or has been removed, or sending is paused for the workspace. The box gives the reason.

    See [How WhatsApp messaging works](/get-started/how-whatsapp-messaging-works).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Meta rejected my template">
    Open the template under **Templates**. Its status is **Rejected** and Meta's reason is shown. Change what the reason points to and choose **Resubmit to Meta**. Meta reviews it again, usually within a few minutes.

    A template marked **Paused** was paused by Meta after poor feedback from customers. The pause lifts by itself after a few hours.

    Owners and admins only. See [Templates overview](/templates/overview) and [Create a template](/templates/create-a-template).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Broadcasts

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="My broadcast stopped">
    A broadcast stops when Meta refuses in a way that would fail for everyone, such as a paused template, a restricted account or a billing problem at Meta. Open the broadcast to see the reason.

    Once it is put right, choose **Resume** to carry on with the people not yet tried. Nobody is sent to twice.

    See [Reach and results](/broadcasts/reach-and-results).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I cannot send a broadcast because of reach">
    Each plan covers a number of different contacts you can broadcast to in a calendar month. A broadcast that would go past it cannot be sent until the month turns or the plan changes.

    See [Reach and results](/broadcasts/reach-and-results) and [Plans](/billing/plans).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## The phone app

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I am not getting notifications on my phone">
    In the app, open the **Settings** tab, then **Notifications**, and choose **Allow notifications**. If your iPhone asks for permission, allow it. If the row says **Off**, tap it to open your iPhone's settings and allow notifications for the app there.

    Then check the switches under **Tell me about**.

    See [Phone notifications](/phone-app/notifications).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The app says I am not in a workspace">
    You need to accept your invitation first. Open the invitation email, choose **Accept invitation**, and join. Then sign in to the app with that same email address.

    Invitations last seven days. If yours has expired, ask the person who invited you for a new one.

    See [Get the phone app](/phone-app/get-the-app).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I cannot find the app for Android">
    The phone app is for iPhone only. There is no Android app. Use the web app at [app.msgcompanion.com](https://app.msgcompanion.com) instead.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## WhatsApp numbers

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="My number will not connect">
    Try these in order.

    - **The Meta window closed before finishing.** Nothing was changed. Choose **Continue with Facebook** and go through it again.
    - **The Meta window never opened, or Companion keeps waiting.** A browser that blocks trackers can stop the window reporting back. Allow facebook.com for Companion, or use another browser, then choose **Start again**.
    - **Meta finished without a phone number.** Try again, and when Meta asks, add or pick the number your business uses.
    - **Your plan's numbers are all connected.** Remove a number you no longer use, or move up a plan.
    - **The number is on ordinary WhatsApp.** Move it to the free WhatsApp Business app first.
    - **Meta did not accept a step.** The message quotes what Meta said. Try again, and if it repeats, send that message to support.

    Owners and admins only. See [Connect your WhatsApp number](/get-started/connect-whatsapp).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My number is connected but messages are not delivered">
    Meta does not deliver chargeable messages until there is a payment method on your WhatsApp Business account. Add a card in Meta's billing settings.

    Then check the number on the **WhatsApp** page. If its health says **Not sending**, choose **Reconnect**. If it says **Paused**, choose **Resume number**. If it says **Restricted**, open WhatsApp Manager to see what Meta is asking for.

    See [Connect your WhatsApp number](/get-started/connect-whatsapp) and [What Meta charges](/billing/meta-charges).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My old chats have not come across">
    Contacts and past chats are copied from the WhatsApp Business app while it is open. Keep the app open on the phone for a few minutes. The number shows **Syncing history** until it finishes.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Plans and billing

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Sending has paused after my free trial">
    When a trial ends, you have three more days to choose a plan. After that, sending pauses: messages, broadcasts and workflows. Messages from customers still arrive and nothing is deleted.

    The owner chooses a plan under **Settings**, **Billing**, and sending starts again. If you are not the owner, ask them.

    The same happens if a payment for your plan fails and the card is not updated within 14 days.

    See [Free trial](/billing/free-trial) and [Manage your plan](/billing/manage-your-plan).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="We have hit a plan limit">
    Nothing is switched off. You keep what you have and cannot add more of that thing until something changes.

    | Limit | What to do |
    | --- | --- |
    | Seats | Add a seat under **Settings**, **Billing**, or remove a member first. |
    | WhatsApp numbers | Remove a number you no longer use, or move up a plan. |
    | Workflows switched on | Pause one, or move up a plan. |
    | Forms | The template is saved as a draft. Move up a plan to submit it. |
    | Integrations | Disconnect one, or move up a plan. |
    | Broadcast reach | Wait for the next calendar month, or move up a plan. |

    Only the owner can change the plan. See [Plans](/billing/plans) and [Manage your plan](/billing/manage-your-plan).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why do I need to add a card at Meta?">
    Meta charges for some WhatsApp messages and bills you directly. It will not deliver chargeable messages without a payment method on your WhatsApp account. See [What Meta charges](/billing/meta-charges).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Getting around

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I cannot see a section a colleague has">
    You only see the sections your role can open. A rep does not have Contacts, Broadcasts or Analytics, and only owners and admins have WhatsApp, Website chat and Integrations. See [Find your way around](/get-started/find-your-way-around) and [Roles](/team/roles).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I hid the setup guide and want it back">
    Open the menu under your name at the bottom of the sidebar and choose **Setup guide**. Owners and admins only. See [Quickstart](/get-started/quickstart).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Something looks broken for everyone">
    Check the [status page](https://status.msgcompanion.com), then [contact support](/help/contact-support).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
