---
title: "Welcome to Companion"
description: "One shared WhatsApp inbox for your whole team, on the web and on your iPhone."
---

Companion is a shared inbox for your business's WhatsApp numbers. Your team answers every customer from one place, and sends products, payment links, forms and templates without leaving the chat.

You keep your number, and you can keep using the WhatsApp Business app on your phone alongside it.
