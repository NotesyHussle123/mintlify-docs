The phone app is built for replying to customers. It has four tabs along the bottom: **Chats**, **Tasks**, **Insights** and **Settings**.

You see the same conversations as on the web. A rep sees their own chats and unassigned ones. Managers, admins and owners see every chat, and each row says who has it.

## Chats

Your inbox. The number on the tab is how many chats have something unread.

- **Search** by typing in the field at the top.
- **Filter** with the filter button: **Unread**, **Assigned to me**, **Unassigned**, **Overdue**, **Awaiting payment**, **Website chats** and **Archived**. You can also narrow by one tag and one WhatsApp number.
- **Swipe a chat left** to mark it **Unread** or to **Archive** it. In the archived list, swipe to **Unarchive**.
- Tap **New chat** to write to a customer first.

Words you typed but did not send show on the chat's row as a draft.

## A conversation

Tap a chat to open it. Type at the bottom and send, as you would in WhatsApp.

The actions button beside the message box opens the tools:

| Tool | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Send product** | Send a product from your connected shop. |
| **Payment link** | Send a link the customer can pay. |
| **Send template** | Send an approved template. |
| **Send location** | Send a map pin, such as a saved place. |
| **Send document** | Send a file from your phone. |
| **Send photo** | Take a photo or choose one. |
| **Look up stock** | Check a product without sending anything. |
| **Saved reply** | Drop a saved reply into the message box. |
| **Add note** | A note for the team. The customer never sees it. |
| **Follow up later** | Set yourself a reminder for this chat. |

You can also:

- Tap the microphone to record a voice note, in WhatsApp chats.
- Reply to a particular message, or react to one.
- Tap a message that did not send to try again.
- Search within the chat, and load earlier messages.
- Tap the customer's name at the top of the chat for their tags, the team's notes, shared photos, links and documents, and their marketing opt-out.
- Open the chat's menu to mark it unread or archive it. A rep can pick up an unassigned chat or release their own. Managers, admins and owners can assign a chat to anyone.

When you cannot type, the message box says why. After 24 hours without a message from the customer, only a template can be sent. If your workspace has typed replies switched off, you can still send voice notes, templates and anything from the tools. See [How WhatsApp messaging works](/get-started/how-whatsapp-messaging-works).

A website chat carries text and photos only, so it has a shorter list of tools.

## New chat

Use this to write to a customer who has not written to you.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the person">
    Tap **New chat** on the **Chats** tab and search for them. The search covers people you already have chats with and customers from your connected shop. If they have a chat already, it opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Or enter a new number">
    Tap **New number**, enter the phone number and a name, and choose which of your WhatsApp numbers to send from.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send a template">
    The chat opens with the template picker. WhatsApp only allows an approved template as the first message.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Tasks

The chats waiting on a reply, payment links to chase, and follow-ups you have set for later under **Upcoming**. Overdue ones come first.

A rep's list is split into chats waiting on them and unassigned chats free to pick up. Managers, admins and owners see everything by day, with the assignee on each row.

Swipe a task left to mark it done. It comes back if something new happens on that chat. Clearing a task on an unassigned chat clears it for the whole team, so the app asks first.

A dot on the tab means a chat has waited a day or more for a reply.

## Insights

Headline figures, charts for **Sales through chat** and **Messages sent**, and written **Highlights**. It shows the last seven days. Tap **Choose dates** to pick other days.

<Note>
Managers, admins and owners only. Reps do not have this tab. If your plan does not include analytics, the tab says so in place of the figures.
</Note>

## Settings

Your card at the top opens **Profile**: your name, email, role and workspace. These are read-only in the app.

| Row | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Workspace** | The business name, its WhatsApp numbers with their status, and the team. Read-only. |
| **Saved places** | The shop or showroom addresses you can send as a map pin. Tap one to see it in Maps. |
| **Notifications** | Which notifications you get. See [Phone notifications](/phone-app/notifications). |
| **Appearance** | **System**, **Light** or **Dark**. |
| **Contact support** and **Email us** | Ways to reach support. |
| **Log out** | Signs you out on this phone only, and stops its notifications. |
| **Delete account** | Removes your profile and signs you out everywhere. Conversations stay with the workspace. An owner is sent to the web, because their account goes with the workspace. |

The foot of the screen shows the email you are signed in with and the app version.

## What you do on the web

The phone app is for conversations. Use the web app to:

- Connect or manage WhatsApp numbers.
- Write templates and forms and submit them to Meta.
- Manage contacts, and send broadcasts.
- Build workflows.
- Set up website chat and integrations.
- Invite people, change roles and manage teams.
- Change your name, turn on two-factor authentication, or add saved places.
- Choose or change a plan. Nothing is sold in the app.
- See the full analytics pages and the audit log.
