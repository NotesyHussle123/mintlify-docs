The phone app sends a notification when a customer writes, so a new message does not wait until you next look.

## Turn notifications on

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the app">
    On the **Chats** tab, a card says **Never miss a customer**. Choose **Turn on**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Allow notifications">
    When your iPhone asks for permission, allow it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If you dismissed the card, open the **Settings** tab and choose **Notifications**, then **Allow notifications**. The row shows **On** when it is working.

## What you are notified about

Open the **Settings** tab and choose **Notifications**. Under **Tell me about**:

| Switch | You hear when |
| --- | --- |
| **New messages** | A customer writes to you on WhatsApp. |
| **Only my chats** | On: chats nobody has been assigned yet are skipped. Off by default. |
| **Website chats** | A visitor writes through your website. |
| **Assigned to you** | A teammate gives you a chat. |
| **Payments received** | A payment link you sent is paid. |

A reminder you set with **Follow up later** always arrives.

Under **How they look**:

- **Show message text**: puts the customer's words on the lock screen. Turn it off to show a neutral line.
- **Sound**: plays a sound when a notification arrives.

The same screen has an **Email** group for the emails Companion sends you.

These choices follow your account. They are the same on the web and on any phone you sign in on.

## Good to know

- While a chat is open on screen, its notifications arrive quietly.
- When a chat is read, on your phone, on the web or by a colleague, its notification is taken off your lock screen.
- The number on the app icon is your unread chats.
- Logging out stops notifications on that phone.

## Fix missing notifications

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The Notifications row says Turn on">
    You have not been asked yet. Open the **Settings** tab, choose **Notifications**, then **Allow notifications**, and allow them when your iPhone asks.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The row says Off, open Settings">
    Notifications were refused for the app. Tap the row to open your iPhone's settings for Message Companion and allow notifications there. The app picks up the change when you come back to it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="It says This phone is not receiving notifications">
    Notifications are allowed, but the phone could not sign up for them. Check your connection, then tap **Retry**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Notifications are on, but some do not arrive">
    Check the switches under **Tell me about**. If **New messages** or **Website chats** is off, you will not hear about those. If **Only my chats** is on, you will not hear about chats nobody has picked up.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="There is no sound, or no message text">
    Check **Sound** and **Show message text** under **How they look**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Still nothing">
    Check that your iPhone's own settings allow notifications for Message Companion. If they do and nothing arrives, [contact support](/help/contact-support) and include the app version from the foot of the **Settings** tab.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Notifications on the web

On the web, the bell lists new messages, payments and chats given to you, and can show desktop notifications. See [Notifications](/conversations/notifications).
